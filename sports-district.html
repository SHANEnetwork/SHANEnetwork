School-approved athlete profiles

In a deployed district, authorized staff would publish approved roster information such as player names, numbers, positions and achievements. This preview deliberately contains no athlete identities or private messages.

Student data, photographs, permissions and opt-outs must be controlled by the schools. Staff-only messaging requires verified roles, restricted access, moderation and audit records before deployment.
