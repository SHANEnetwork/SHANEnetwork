Accept payments on your website

SHANE's guide for people creating their own business websites.

  1. Choose a payment provider such as Stripe Checkout or PayPal Business.
  2. Create your own account and complete the provider's identity and payout verification. A corporation or LLC is not necessarily required; sole proprietors can often apply.
  3. Set up a product, price, refund policy, and secure checkout in the provider's dashboard.
  4. Use the provider's approved payment link or hosted checkout integration. Never place secret API keys in publicly visible website HTML.
  5. Test checkout and verify the payment server-side before delivering a paid product.
  6. Check fees, taxes, and applicable business registration requirements for your location.

Important: SHANE does not ask for your card details, banking password, or payment-provider secret keys. Each business owner should receive funds directly into their own verified payment account.
