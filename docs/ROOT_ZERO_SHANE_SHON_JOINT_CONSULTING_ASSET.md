## 1. Joint proposition and boundaries

**SHANE: From idea to endeavor.** An AI-first, permissioned collaboration network for ideas, people, organizations, events, services, projects and enterprises. The endeavor, its identity, history, revisions, members, relationships and outcomes persist independently of any website. A person describes a goal; SHANE interprets, proposes actual capabilities, builds a working result, saves it, remembers it, and resumes with the person later. Creation, discovery, collaboration and operation are one continuous experience. People can also join existing endeavors without creating sites or using AI. Manual controls are optional, not mandatory configuration homework.

**SHON: People, places, possibilities.** A separately operated, free, people-centered voluntary shared-home, household, companionship, resource and mutual-support network, NOT merely another rental marketplace. Preserve four welcoming entry points: "I need a place"; "I have space to share"; "I can offer help"; "I'm looking for someone". A participant may offer and need things at once. Do not turn SHON's mission into paid ranking, ordinary property sales/rent ads, coercive eligibility or unverified emergency-response promises.

**Complementary rather than merged.** SHANE may offer optional capability adapters to SHON's independent organization and users. Neither brand, production database, identity provider, membership, housing details nor governance may be silently merged. SHON's effectiveness does not depend on SHANE uptime. Any future claim that SHANE may contribute 20% of qualifying gross revenue to SHON is a proposed, contingent policy, not a transfer, a current contractual commitment or verified revenue.

### Sharp differentiation statement

- SHANE is **not** just another website builder or social feed. Its product test is whether one endeavor survives creation, real capability use, reflection, return and accepted collaboration with all components under one authorized identity.
- SHON is **not** a conventional pay-to-find housing listing service. Its product test is whether two consenting people can safely explore a compatible voluntary shared-living or supportive relationship, understand realistic terms, communicate privately, and freely leave, while preserving privacy and accessibility.
- The advantage is a hypothesis to validate against alternatives, **not a market leadership claim**. If real journeys do not work and users do not return, beautiful pages and broad features do not establish product-market fit.

## 4. The one demo that must convince a new visitor

### SHANE: bakery endeavor, not a bakery webpage

Input: "I want to start a small bakery, have recipes, about $500 and a friend who might help, and I'd like to sell at local festivals."

Minimum live demonstration, without inventing festivals/members:
1. Preserve the exact raw vision. Create one owned private endeavor ID; subsequent refresh/retry does not duplicate.
2. Show identified goals, known resources and unknowns; offer relevant *real* network listings or say that none are available.
3. Create and persist an editable first artifact (launch plan or private public-page draft), linked to the same endeavor.
4. Let the person request a change conversationally and observe the *actual persisted artifact* update, with revision history/undo.
5. Ask before publishing or inviting. Invite a dedicated second test account, require acceptance, establish a private conversation.
6. Log out, return on another device/browser, restore the same endeavor, all components, actual discussions and permissions.
7. Verify public page is independently crawlable only after explicit publish; private drafts and account data must not be indexed.

### SHON: two real-consent testers, synthetic profiles

Two dedicated accounts use separate browser profiles and synthetic household preferences. Opt-in public Discover reveals only approved fields. Private story and optional answers stay private. Request, accept and message; decline and block must prevent unauthorized writes at the database, not just conceal a button. Confirm persistence, opt-out, reentry, mobile layout, keyboard navigation and clear agreement/exit terms. Do not use actual housing users as test fixtures.

## 5. Strategic design decisions for maximum genuine interest

**Choose one compelling first-use win**, not 25 disconnected features. SHANE: establish something useful in a single conversation, reopen it, and connect one genuine collaborator. SHON: find a relevant consented conversation or a clearly identified community resource without paying, mandatory phone access, or surrendering private details. Show an honest no-match experience rather than fabricate listings.

**Trust and clarity outrank artificial growth.** Explain what AI did versus suggested, live versus planned, verified versus self-reported. Provide clear privacy states and export/exit controls. Use warm image-forward original artwork and mobile-first accessible controls; never require a giant intake form merely to start.

**Small, authentic networks first.** Candidate first SHANE cohorts: community organizers, a craft vendor/festival cohort, independent service professionals, student-led collaboration with appropriate age safeguards, and a nonprofit/civic organization. Candidate SHON cohorts: willing homeshare hosts/seekers and independent community organizations with actual regional coverage. These are outreach hypotheses, **not signed partners or guaranteed supply**. Do not solicit private housing records or send outreach automatically.

**Discoverability is a feature.** Public opt-in endeavor/listing pages need server-rendered or reliably crawlable HTML, unique titles, canonical URLs, accessible headings, useful structured data where valid, and a public sitemap. Private profiles, AI conversation histories, drafts and message rooms require authenticated access plus no-index protection. A robots file alone is not data security.

**Retention through meaningful progress.** A return visitor should see "What moved forward since your last session?" with truthful completed operations, collaborator response (if authorized), next useful actions and undo. Record continuity and trust rather than maximizing time-on-site.

**Distribution without premature ads:** use sharable opt-in endeavor summaries, partner invitations, local events/civic organizations, search-friendly public resources, and examples with explicit demo labels. No fabricated testimonials, matches or metrics.

## 7. Engineering architecture and isolation contract

SHANE persistent endeavor = ID + owner + exact user vision + privacy state + conversation/revisions + typed artifacts + linked participants/roles + task/event history + explicit approval receipts. Operations need immutable idempotency keys, owner scope, authorization and rollback. A shared UI conversation is a *command surface* over real services, not several disconnected local-session facades.

SHON remains an independently deployed application and database. A future adapter should accept minimal scoped inputs such as public non-sensitive endeavor summary, explicit SHON participant permission, purpose, expiry, revocation token and audit ID; it must not copy private story, medical conditions, full household details or confidential messages. Prefer a versioned capability contract and actual consent to federation or shared database tables. Failure of SHANE must not prevent SHON from loading.

Detangler applies at data/application boundaries and catches duplicate records, orphaned creations, wrong-endeavor associations, ghost collaboration permissions, navigation loops, missing assets, database-policy leakage and stalled tasks. No new rule is considered accepted until tests prove it helps and does not regress behavior.
