## 0. Two products, complementary missions
**SHONnetwork (Supported Housing Opportunity Network)**: an already-developed v1 people/places/possibilities network to uncover and responsibly connect underused housing, homeshare, room-and-board, caretaking, temporary and long-term housing arrangements, housing-stability partners and mutual support. Keep the v1 product independently useful, with its own infrastructure, identity, governance, data and safety rules. The v1 source's release audit still asks for evidence of optional profile saving, consent-aware matching, discover/messaging/block/report flows, and keyboard/mobile accessibility; “v1 finished” and “every user journey independently verified” are distinct claims.

**SHANEnetwork (Shared Human Ambitions, Networks & Enterprises)**: an endeavor-centered creation and collaboration network, not merely an AI builder. The person explains an ambition conversationally; one permissioned persistent endeavor identity connects its goals, creative and practical products, changes, resources, collaborators, history and optional public presence. People can also discover/join an existing endeavor without making a website. Do not call deterministic field filling real AI orchestration or feature stubs finished.

**Complementarity without dependency**: an independently governed SHON endeavor may *opt in* to SHANE's general-purpose capability services. SHANE must never require SHON signup; SHON must never require SHANE uptime. No unsolicited profile replication, no shared service-role keys, no automatic housing-listing import, no merging private participant data.

## 1. Audience and positioning hypotheses
- SHON **first demand**: people needing a trustworthy room/home arrangement, including those with low/no income, relocation, students, older adults, families, people needing compatibility or accessibility features; exclude nobody merely for lack of formal diagnosis or unusual housing need. **First supply**: willing hosts, property owners, faith/civic institutions, homeshare programs, compatible mutual-support partners, verified unused properties/rooms. Prioritize actual, realistically eligible listings over a visually full but empty directory. Never promise free homes, guaranteed safety or outcomes.
- SHANE **first demand**: people starting small collective endeavors: a bakery or craft fair, neighborhood concert, volunteer project, club, community organization, research collaboration, small service enterprise. **First supply**: actual discoverable collaborators, existing organizations, relevant capabilities and events. Make “join and help” as prominent as “create.” Avoid a feature menu as the entry step.
- Shared message: **SHON helps people find conditions for stable living. SHANE helps people and groups build and advance what they want to accomplish.** A consented bridge can connect useful resources without entangling identities.

## 2. Competitive analysis (external provider claims checked 2026-11-23; no adoption or price assumptions)

**Interpretation:** These are adjacent service models, not a claim that we outperform them. The largest strategic threat is not a single competitor; it is trying to launch everything simultaneously without one repeated, satisfying journey, sufficient real supply and reliable trust mechanisms.

## 3. Three mutually competitive development strategies (run bounded Ponderer–Harmonizer–Optimizer passes)
**A. SHANE “one endeavor, fully real” (recommended first)**: choose a bakery/club sample; real-account describe → create → find an actual optional collaborator → produce and save useful component → sign out → return to full preserved context → invite/accept in second account → private communications → revise. Launch only when every gate has evidence. Advantage: durable differentiation and measurable acceptance. Disadvantage: narrower public feature rollout.
**B. SHON “one housing-stability journey, fully safe” (recommended parallel)**: search verified eligible room/homeshare → contact under consent controls → save member preferences → message → manage block/report → agree expectations → either continue or exit, without lost data. Advantage: direct immediate value and supply-building. Disadvantage: needs partner/host pipeline and careful safeguards.
**C. Network growth first**: public profiles, campaigns, SEO, partnerships and event content before real private journeys. Advantage: early awareness. Disadvantage: creates empty engagement and overstates product readiness. Adopt only narrowly to bring first legitimate listings/members after A/B gate progress.

Convergence rule: give A and B priority, borrow targeted discovery, warmth, orientation and retention experiments from C; do not substitute C for failed signed-in journey. Each proposer supplies baseline, user value, tests, cost, privacy impact, rollback, and evidence; compare no more than two bounded iterations before deciding.

## 5. Product craft and adoption experiments
- First 60 seconds: show a vivid, accessible honest invitation. “What would you like to accomplish?” for SHANE, “What sort of home arrangement might work?” for SHON; allow **browse without an account** where privacy-safe, and offer one clear next step, not a questionnaire wall.
- Narrative/interface: original artwork, welcoming human language, warm customizable palette, obvious return-to-your-endeavor, persistent progress trail. Avoid blue-only enterprise UI, auto-playing media that traps focus, or mandatory phone verification.
- True moments of value: SHANE returns a *working saved artifact*, not only a generated paragraph; SHON returns *genuine discoverable options* or accurately says none are available.
- Demand/supply seeding: start with a geographically and operationally credible pilot, local partners and verified entries before national marketing. Partner-assisted onboarding may be better than cold generic acquisition.
- Measurement with privacy: first-action completion, account-backed return, invite acceptance, successful permission-safe message, real match progress, relevant published search impressions and accessible task success. Never fabricate numbers.
- Revenue: free essential discovery/onboarding initially; consider opted-in professional services or organizations only after demonstrated value, sustainability, consent and legal review. Existing SHANE plan's contemplated percentage of qualifying future revenue supporting SHON is an intention, not a transfer or financial guarantee.
