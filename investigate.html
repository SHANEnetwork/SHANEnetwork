State government offices and registries

For criminal or incident records, look up the relevant local police or sheriff records office through its official government website. Access, eligibility, accuracy and permitted use of records vary by jurisdiction. For support, choose national or local informational resources according to your needs, but independently evaluate them before sharing sensitive details.

For a company, search its official state business registry and applicable professional licensing board. For housing, independently confirm the property and the person's authority to offer it. An identity document alone does not prove ownership, authority, or honesty.
