# SHANE: Non-negotiable product vision

## Foundational identity: central endeavor collaboration network

**SHANEnetwork is an endeavor-centered collaboration network, not primarily an AI website builder.** The **SHANE Network** is the central hub where people and endeavors discover one another, form relationships, coordinate activity, and collaborate. Specialized hubs connect into this central Network; they must not become isolated silos.

- An endeavor exists independently of any website, AI conversation, business registration, or specialized hub.
- People can join, discover, participate, and collaborate without creating a website or using AI.
- Endeavor identity, membership, consent, permissions, history, and relationships remain anchored in the central Network across all connected hubs.
- SHANE intelligence helps interpret ambitions, find opportunities, organize collaboration, and operate supported capabilities. Website creation is one optional capability among many.
- Every feature and AI operation should strengthen or preserve the central Network's ability to connect people and endeavors. Do not make website-building the default prerequisite.
- Keep SHANEnetwork and the SHANE Network distinct from the separate SHONnetwork initiative.

## One conversational intelligence, two complementary experiences

**SHANE creation experience:** A person describes a website, project, association, club, business, research effort, or other ambition. SHANE interprets the conversation, builds and configures supported capabilities, preserves the evolving endeavor, and helps revise it. A website is optional.

**SHANE Network experience:** People describe the endeavors they want to establish, join, support, or collaborate on. SHANE helps them discover people and existing endeavors, organize memberships and permissions, invite collaborators with consent, communicate, coordinate activities, and grow their work. The central endeavor identity is shared across specialized hubs and created websites.

**No disconnected duplication:** Creating a website for an endeavor must not silently create a second unrelated endeavor. Starting from Network discovery must not require a website. Both entry points converge on the same owner-scoped endeavor and permissioned relationships.

**Implementation requirement:** Conversational AI should operate actual supported services with verified, persistent results and appropriate approvals, rather than only suggest pages, fill forms, or redirect users to unrelated tools. Manual access remains optional. Distinguish planned operations from executed actions.

## Discovery and collaboration start during creation

When a person describes a new website, club, association, project, business, or other endeavor, SHANE should offer relevant **existing Network endeavors, organizations, people, and collaboration opportunities from the beginning**, not only after publication. The user may choose to create something new, join something existing, or do both.

- Search only legitimately discoverable Network records, respect privacy and account permissions, and distinguish confirmed matches from suggestions or inferred fit.
- Explain why a result may be relevant to the person's stated aims, capabilities, location preferences, and collaboration interests; provide a route to inspect the real listing.
- Allow conversational refinement of discovery goals and results, including exploration of specific organizations and collaborators.
- Connect opt-in introductions, collaboration invitations, messaging, and shared workspaces to the same persistent endeavor identity.
- Never automatically contact others, join a group, publish a listing, or disclose private interests without the person's approval.
- Keep discovery usable independently of website creation; make it available within the builder and central Network.

## Collective opportunity and member-led organizations

SHANEnetwork must support not only individuals creating websites or projects but people **finding one another and organizing collective endeavors**: professional associations, peer groups, guilds, coalitions, clubs, trade networks, cooperatives, worker organizations and unions. People may join several endeavors with different roles and permissions.

The SHANE Network should support discovery by profession, interests, goals, location, and collaboration intent; member invitations and approval; governance and membership choices; private discussion and shared workspaces; proposals, voting or decision processes where configured; joint projects and events; and negotiated opportunities such as collective advertising, purchasing, shared services, event access, and exploration of group insurance. Specialized hubs and websites plug into the same endeavor and member relationship graph.

SHANE should help members explore feasible arrangements and coordinate work, but never promise negotiated rates, insurance eligibility, event invitations, legal recognition, or other third-party outcomes. Financial, insurance, employment, and collective-bargaining arrangements require applicable legal and regulatory review and explicit participant authorization. Protect private membership and association information with purpose-limited permissions and consent. No automatic public exposure or outreach.

## Example: independent SHONnetwork association connected to SHANE Network

SHONnetwork is a separate housing-stability and mutual-support initiative, not another name for SHANEnetwork or the SHANE Network. If its owner elects to establish a SHONnetwork association on SHANE Network, it should operate as an independently governed endeavor that connects to the central Network and specialized hubs **without altering SHONnetwork's separate website, infrastructure, or records without explicit authorization**.

Its potential scope includes homelessness prevention and response; aging in place and shared property upkeep; mutual support, companionship and compatible housemates; support needs whether or not formally medically recognized; temporary or transitional housing for relocation and rebuilding stability; and connecting people with willing hosts, property owners, organizations, and resources. Eligibility and discovery should not presume homelessness, diagnosis, age, or income level.

Housing matches require consent, privacy controls, appropriate verification, safeguarding, clear expectations and exit rights. SHANE can assist with discovery and coordination, but must not imply a match, accommodation, or service has been guaranteed.

## Housing stability through boarders and shared homes

A SHONnetwork association's housing-stability work may help an existing resident avoid displacement by finding a compatible paying boarder or housemate. The person offering a room may be the person at risk: growing property taxes, insurance, utilities, repairs, rent, or other costs can threaten continued residence. A fair room-and-board arrangement can create income for the resident and housing for the boarder, sometimes with mutually agreed companionship or practical help.

Support resident-led listings as well as conventional housing seekers. Distinguish rent, shared expenses, household assistance, and companionship clearly; do not presume unpaid care, medical support, or an obligation to provide services. Check permission to sublet or host, relevant occupancy and housing rules, accessibility needs, compatibility, written terms, safety, privacy, and fair exit arrangements. Never promise that a boarder will solve financial insecurity or prevent displacement.

## Universal and impartial review

SHANE offers the same neutral safety checklist to everyone entering consequential arrangements. It does not single out, rank, profile, or imply suspicion about particular people or organizations. Encourage routine review of all contracts, written terms, lawful verification of identity and authority, and careful payment checks. Background screening is not universally appropriate or lawful; consider purpose, jurisdiction, consent, consumer-reporting, fair-housing, employment, and anti-discrimination requirements. Discovery matches indicate relevance, not trustworthiness. Informational resource links are not endorsements. SHANE does not certify character or provide legal services.

## SHANE-wide counterparty investigation and pre-signature agreement review

SHANE should offer a reusable, optional **Investigate and Review Before Committing** capability across endeavors, professional associations, purchasing groups, business relationships, services, and SHONnetwork housing arrangements.

Before a person signs, pays, shares sensitive data, or enters an arrangement, SHANE should help them (1) verify the claimed identity, affiliation, ownership or authority of the other party using consent-based verification and lawful, reliable sources; (2) investigate relevant documented inconsistencies, fraud indicators, payment requests, and conflicts of interest; (3) review the actual contract and supporting documents for financial exploitation, abusive or one-sided obligations, hidden fees, coercive restrictions, liability shifts, termination barriers, privacy and data-use risks, and missing protections; (4) explain findings in accessible language with cited evidence, clause references, uncertainty, and concrete questions or proposed revisions; and (5) preserve a private, user-controlled review record where authorized.

The reviewer must distinguish verified facts, allegations, unanswered questions, and legal conclusions; never invent background-check results, guarantee trustworthiness, label someone abusive without adequate evidence, or claim legal review is a substitute for qualified jurisdiction-specific counsel. Follow applicable screening, consumer protection, privacy, anti-discrimination, and consent requirements. Do not contact, expose, accuse, or report a person or organization automatically. Protect both parties' legitimate interests and permit correction of errors.

The capability should be accessible during the conversational creation and collaboration journey, **before** any binding commitment. SHONnetwork can reuse it without altering SHON's independent infrastructure absent authorization.

## Universal, non-discriminatory due diligence

SHANE should offer the same pre-commitment review guidance to **everyone**, regardless of the identity or perceived characteristics of the counterparty. It must not select, label, or single out individuals or organizations for investigation based on unsupported suspicion, protected characteristics, or inferred trustworthiness. It should encourage all participants to read agreements, verify relevant authority and identity, and consider only appropriate, lawful, consent-based background checks where applicable.

SHANE provides informational resources and review tools, not endorsements, trust ratings, character judgments, legal services, or guarantees. Official registries, police records offices, national support resources, and other external links are informational starting points, not assurances of safety or integrity. Screening and records access must respect purpose restrictions, fair housing, employment, privacy, consumer-reporting law, and applicable consent requirements.

## One-sentence contract
**The person describes the endeavor they imagine. SHANE designs, writes, builds, configures, and refines the actual website and its supported capabilities; the person guides the vision and approves important decisions.**

## The primary experience
1. **Vision:** Invite a free-form description, including uncertain and unconventional ideas. Do not demand technical selections.
2. **Interpretation:** SHANE identifies audience, purpose, tone, pages, visuals, content, and potentially useful capabilities. Distinguish confirmed requirements from possibilities and questions.
3. **Creation:** SHANE produces a visually rich, responsive, editable, working website draft and configures supported capabilities itself. Show the real preview.
4. **Conversation:** The person says what to change in ordinary language. SHANE updates the draft, preserves unaffected work, and explains material changes.
5. **Publication:** Save and publish only with explicit user action. Preserve project ownership, security, and rollback/undo paths.

## Product rules
- **No configuration homework:** Do not make the person choose a template, layout, colors, page count, modules, databases, or integrations before SHANE can begin. Advanced editing may exist as an optional escape hatch.
- **AI does the work:** The primary action is to describe a vision, then see SHANE build it. Do not substitute a checklist, generic proposal, mock button, or an unimplemented feature for a working website.
- **Rich, distinct design:** Aim for warm, welcoming, expressive, professional websites with appropriate imagery, hierarchy, typography, depth, and responsive layout. Avoid a one-size-fits-all corporate/admin appearance.
- **Function follows purpose:** A festival may need event details, volunteer coordination, registrations, and updates. A club may need membership and discussions. A shop may need catalog and checkout. SHANE should infer needs and provision only capabilities actually supported by its system.
- **Honest capabilities:** A link to a shared SHANE service is not an automatically created, endeavor-specific forum or registration system. Never claim it is.
- **Respect uncertainty:** A possible farmers market is not a confirmed central feature. Ask concise questions only when the answer is essential or high-stakes; otherwise make revisable choices.
- **Keep continuity:** Preserve existing decisions, writing, styling, data, and working features across revisions. Never overwrite SHONnetwork while working on SHANEnetwork.
- **Protect users:** Avoid inventing real-world facts, fake testimonials, fabricated dates or prices, or publishing without consent. Keep private member information protected.

## Definition of a meaningful milestone
A real person can describe an idea, receive an attractive editable website draft, request a change conversationally, see it reflected in the live preview, save the project, reload it without losing choices, and publish deliberately. Each promised module must be demonstrably functional and associated with the correct endeavor.

## Implementation status note
This document is the product contract, **not a claim that all features are implemented**. Current AI generation depends on account access and a configured provider; network links are not yet automatically provisioned private community services. Prioritize closing those gaps over adding more setup controls.

## Invisible implementation and complete creative continuity
- **Conversation is the workspace:** People must never have to copy chat text into setup forms. SHANE translates natural-language collaboration into internal project models, populated configuration, content, and working implementation offscreen. Advanced editing is optional.
- **No silent truncation:** Never silently discard conversation, instructions, pages, revisions, or project material because of token, storage, page-count, or UI limits. Store full source material durably; use indexed retrieval, summaries, and bounded working context only as derived aids, never as replacements for originals. When a technical limit prevents an operation, explain it and preserve the original unchanged.
- **Continuity across sessions:** Save the conversation, evolving project specification, generated assets, approved decisions, and version history together, with explicit privacy and ownership controls. A browser session alone is not durable project storage.
- **Portable ownership:** Support persistent online projects and downloadable, usable exports; local-device persistence may supplement but must not be misrepresented as cloud backup. Clearly distinguish draft, saved, exported, and published states.
- **Automatic orchestration:** SHANE should select and connect already supported messaging, community, collaboration, and other capabilities based on the endeavor's needs, without asking users to configure them. Verify that each integration works for that specific endeavor before representing it as available.
- **Never confuse an implementation plan with completion:** These are mandatory product requirements, not claims that current SHANE already implements them.

## Reuse and adaptation of existing SHANE programs
- Treat existing SHANE programs, page structures, forms, database schemas, and supported network capabilities as reusable implementation foundations, not questionnaires that people must complete.
- Interpret the person's conversation into structured, validated project data. Automatically populate the underlying form fields and configurations, then save those structures and generated content without requiring manual transcription.
- For each endeavor, instantiate and adapt supported components (membership, conversations, messages, meetings, and others only where actually implemented and verified), keeping each project's configuration and data isolated.
- On subsequent conversational requests, modify only the affected project configuration, content, or scoped component while preserving unrelated data and working functionality. Keep revisions reversible.
- Shared program changes must be separately tested and deployed; do not let an endeavor-specific request silently rewrite shared production code or other people's projects.
- Persist the complete conversation and project history alongside the structured state; form fields and derived summaries must not become the only surviving record.
