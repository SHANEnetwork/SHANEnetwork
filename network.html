Begin with a vision, not a business plan

What would you like to make possible? It can be a scientific question, a neighborhood initiative, an artwork, a new organization, a shared undertaking, or something that does not yet have a name. You do not need funding, a team, or a finished plan to begin.

SHANE helps you move from an idea to people, opportunities, collaboration, and a working endeavor. You choose what to share and what to keep private.

Your ambition belongs to you

Explore public opportunities without signing in. Create a member account when you want to save private goals or contact collaborators. You decide what becomes public. A shared endeavor should begin with mutual consent, not automatic exposure or pressure to monetize.

SHANE aims to make its essential discovery and collaboration tools accessible without requiring a purchase. Any future paid services should be clearly identified before someone commits.
