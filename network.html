Begin with your vision, then discover together

What would you like to make possible? It can be a scientific question, a neighborhood initiative, an artwork, a new organization, a shared undertaking, or something that does not yet have a name. You do not need funding, a team, or a finished plan to begin.

SHANE helps you move from an idea to people, opportunities, collaboration, and a working endeavor. You choose what to share and what to keep private.
