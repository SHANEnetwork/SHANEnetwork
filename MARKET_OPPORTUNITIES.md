# SHANE market coverage and reusable community product priorities

**Slogan:** One flexible network engine, thousands of possible uses.

## High-opportunity customer families

1. **Membership associations and chambers:** trade groups, professional societies, guilds, unions, industry councils, alumni associations, conferences and regional chapters. Member discovery, chapters, events, resource libraries, mentoring and retention.
2. **Faith and service networks:** congregations, church networks, denominations, interfaith groups, ministries, mutual-aid groups and volunteer coalitions. Local groups, events, messaging, volunteers and resource coordination. Operator chooses policies.
3. **Hobby, maker and creator communities:** bakers, cooks, gardeners, artists, musicians, writers, photographers, tabletop and online gamers, craftspeople, collectors, auto clubs, pet enthusiasts and outdoor recreation.
4. **Neighborhood and residential communities:** homeowner and condominium associations, co-ops, neighborhood groups, block clubs, residents' councils and local mutual-aid circles. Announcements, interest groups, neighbor connections and activities; not a property-management or rental product.
5. **Education and lifelong learning:** alumni networks, university societies, parent groups, adult learning circles, language exchanges, tutoring communities, libraries, maker spaces, school-approved clubs and sports leagues. Student access requires operator-configured safeguards and technical enforcement.
6. **Business and commercial networks:** local merchants, business improvement districts, franchises, suppliers, farmers markets, trade fairs, co-marketing collectives, entrepreneurs, freelancers and coworking groups.
7. **Healthcare and wellness communities:** fitness clubs, sports clubs, walking groups, wellness educators and condition-support communities, with special handling for sensitive data and claims.
8. **Civic and nonprofit organizations:** charities, foundations, environmental groups, neighborhood organizers, volunteer teams, disaster recovery networks and community service projects.
9. **Science and innovation:** independent researchers, inventors, laboratories, citizen-science networks, open-source communities and academic collaborators.
10. **Events and entertainment:** festivals, conventions, fan clubs, performing arts groups, community theaters, concerts and tournaments.
11. **Career and workforce communities:** apprenticeships, mentorship circles, professional development groups, veteran networks and industry transition communities.
12. **International and cultural networks:** immigrant associations, cultural societies, diaspora communities, language groups, international chapters and travel clubs.

## Shared product modules, not vertical forks

Identity and opt-in profiles; organizations and chapters; invitations and role management; member discovery; topic rooms; mutual connections; pager inbox; event calendar and RSVPs; resources; volunteer/task boards; collaborations; shared promotions; organization-branded pages; payments only when reliable accounting and consent are ready; accessibility; privacy and moderation controls.

## Practical order

**Now:** owner-managed organizations, invitations, member search, acceptance and basic member lists. **Next:** reliable organization roles and permission tests, rooms with member-only RLS, reporting and blocking, then pager messages. **Then:** events, chapters, branded templates and deployment configuration. **Later:** commercial subscriptions and integration partnerships.

## Market evidence and caution

ASAE's 2026 State of Associations report cites member retention and engagement as a leading challenge. GrowthZone's 2026 survey highlights member engagement and technology adoption. Foundation for Community Association Research estimates 373,000 U.S. residential community associations at year-end 2025. These are *adjacent market indicators*, not SHANE customer counts, adoption forecasts or revenue projections.

## Guardrails

SHANE's network is broader than a website builder or a business platform. SHON remains separate. No live minor-oriented messaging until an operator-controlled and enforced privacy/safety model is verified. Do not advertise incomplete features as operational. No speculative claims of customer adoption or revenue.
