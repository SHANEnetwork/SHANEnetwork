# SHANE Network: Commercial and Social Mission Boundary

Status: product policy and architectural direction, not an activated billing system.

## SHANEnetwork: commercial engine
- Public discovery of published destinations remains free to browse.
- Paid offerings may include website creation/hosting, advanced AI services, operating tools, organization administration, plugins, promotion, and commercial integrations.
- The exact prices and entitlements are proposals until approved, implemented, and enforced server-side.
- External website owners can connect compatible destinations without surrendering their website ownership; applicable network fees should be consistent with comparable SHANE-hosted members.
- Never claim billing, paid entitlements, or subscription enforcement is live before payment integration and verification.
- Never publish a private endeavor, charge a user, or enable a plugin's privileged access without appropriate authorization.

## SHONnetwork: free people-centered mission
- SHON's purpose is compatible people finding one another to form and sustain shared homes, households, extended families, companionship, and communities through voluntary relationships.
- SHON is not a rental marketplace.
- SHON's essential access and participation should remain free to the people it serves.
- SHON may reuse SHANE-compatible technology without becoming a paid SHANE destination or exposing SHON members to commercial participation requirements.
- SHON user information, membership, and permissions must not be merged into SHANE by default.

## Relationship and accounting
- The proposed commitment is 20% of SHANE gross revenue to support SHON, subject to appropriate governance, accounting, and a sustainable implementation.
- Keep SHANE and SHON projects, databases, privacy boundaries, and deployment workflows distinct.
- A shared protocol means compatible interfaces, not shared ownership of data or unrestricted cross-network access.
- No transfer, donation accounting, or recurring billing is implied to have occurred by this policy document.

## Product experience
- SHANE should accept a vision in ordinary language and handle the technical configuration, preserving human approval of consequential actions.
- Each member destination can have independent administration, membership policies, visibility, and appropriate integrations.
- Build reusable, versioned interfaces for identity, destination discovery, permissions, membership, messaging, and plugins; treat these as planned protocol work until actually implemented and tested.
